What the product checks

R1 looks at the payment path from the budget owner's point of view

The goal is not to “give the agent a wallet.” The goal is to know where a service is already safe for paid agent use and where money, access, or data are still at risk.

Access

Who can approve payment

The audit checks whether there is a clear rule for approval, budget limits, human escalation, and actions the agent must not perform alone.

Money

Price, limit, and stop condition

The service should expose the price before action, a spending limit, and a way to stop a purchase before charging or renewing access.

Proof

Receipt, status page, or transaction

After payment, there should be a verifiable trace: a receipt, status page, public transaction, or another proof that does not expose private data.

Log

What can be checked later

The report records key events: who requested the action, what was paid for, what result was delivered, and where the proof lives.

Revoke

How access can be removed or narrowed

The audit checks whether tokens can be disabled, limits lowered, keys rotated, access closed, or recurring charges stopped.

Result

What the buyer receives

The delivery is not just “payment succeeded.” It should be a report, link, PR, file, access grant, or another artifact that can be checked.

What the client receives

A short audit, not access to private systems

R1 is intentionally small and verifiable: a human-readable report, a structured automation summary, an authority map, and a practical gap list.

01

Audit report

A short document showing what is ready, where the risk is, what to fix first, and which step can safely involve an agent.

02

JSON summary

A structured result for a CRM or agent layer: status, risks, links, and the next allowed step.

03

Authority map

Who approves payment, which limits apply, what data the agent sees, and how quickly access can be revoked.

04

Gap list

A practical list: missing price, missing receipt, unclear limit, missing log, weak revocation, or unverified delivery.

R1 flow

From public signal to verifiable delivery

The workflow is shaped as a CRM process: public evidence and free value first, accepted scope next, payment and delivery only after that.

What the project does not do

no custody
Blocked

Private keys

Custody
